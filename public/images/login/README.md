`oil-landscape-background.png` was generated using the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-12, following the user's request for an oil-painting background behind the login card. ### Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-viewport background behind a centered white corporate login card. Generate a new original landscape oil painting, wide 16:9 composition. Elevated panoramic view of a tropical Indonesian industrial valley, forested green hills on both edges, a winding river, distant blue-grey mountains, a modest modern mineral-processing complex with blue-grey roofs toward the lower right. Overcast atmospheric sky, soft natural daylight, muted deep greens, slate blue, earthy ochre. Clearly hand-painted oil-on-canvas character, visible tasteful brushwork and subtle impasto, rich painterly texture rather than a photograph or digital 3D rendering. Calm, sophisticated operational setting. Broad scenic detail should remain interesting around the outer edges because the middle will be covered by a large login card; no central focal object. Landscape image only: no interface, no login card, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no people, no dramatic fire or disaster. Do not depict a specific real facility. Intended to be darkened slightly with a CSS overlay in the website.
