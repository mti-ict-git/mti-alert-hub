# MTI Connect logo - handshake revision

Two people extend their arms toward a central clasp, representing collaboration and trust. The upright bodies and descending arms suggest an M; the full MTI name remains explicit in the wordmark. This is an original application identity, separate from the official corporate logo.

Colors: Copper #B24F26 and Graphite #303D49. A white separation line keeps the overlapping hands distinct.
Tagline: Terhubung. Terinformasi. Terlindungi.
